Conductor

A contactless fare-payment stack, built end to end.

A mock fare card proves its identity with an SSH certificate. The gate verifies it, challenges the card for proof of possession, and queues every accepted tap locally before syncing it to the API — so a dropped connection never drops a fare.

  • 01

    CA-signed SSH certificates with validity windows and key-strength checks.

  • 02

    A nonce challenge proves the card actually holds its private key.

  • 03

    A SQLite outbox with retries, backoff, and ban-list caching at the gate.

About

I’m most interested in the parts of a system that are easy to ignore until they fail.

Curious about the whole stack.

I’m at home in the connection layer, the retry queue, and the log line that tells you what actually happened.

My projects tend to start as a small itch — a network that roams at the wrong moment, a garden that needs watering, a chat bridge between two communities — and grow into an excuse to learn a new protocol or design a cleaner backend.

I care about software that degrades gracefully: offline queues, bounded retries, clear failure modes, and enough observability to debug it at 2 a.m.
